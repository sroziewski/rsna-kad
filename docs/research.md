# RSNA Knee Abnormality Detection – research notes (2026-09-18)

Legend: **[V]** verified from a primary source · **[P]** second-hand (participant repo quoting the Kaggle Data/Overview/Discussion pages, which are JS-rendered and could not be fetched directly) · **[?]** unverified / inferred. Deadline 2026-10-22 (entry 2026-10-15).

## 1. Facts

### Host statements and data provenance
- **[V]** RSNA press release ([rsna.org](https://www.rsna.org/news/2026/august/ai-challenge-knee-mri)): >5,000 knee MRI exams with reports, "a dozen" languages, 16 sites on five continents; "the dataset used to assess model performance was annotated by expert radiologists"; training labels come from real reports "not neatly organized in a table". Co-leaders: Po-Hao Chen and Naveen Subhas (Cleveland Clinic). $77,000 pool incl. a new efficiency category; winners announced Nov 2026 (RSNA 2026, Chicago). The RSNA challenge page says 19 sites ([rsna.org](https://www.rsna.org/artificial-intelligence/ai-image-challenge/knee-mri-ai-challenge)) – count is inconsistent across RSNA pages.
- **[P]** Ground truth (from the Kaggle Data page as quoted by [homeshwarnelakurthi/STRATEGY.md](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/homeshwarnelakurthi/RSNA-Knee-Abnormality-Detection/main/docs/STRATEGY.md)): labels are **image-derived, not report-derived** – two subspecialty MSK radiologists read the images, a third adjudicated; "ambiguous or borderline findings were graded as negative to favour specificity". Host: "if image interpretation and report text disagree, the image-derived label should be considered authoritative." Severity thresholds quoted: ACL ">50% of fibres disrupted" or discontinuity (mild signal change = negative); meniscus = signal "definitely contacting the meniscal surface on >=2 images" (intrasubstance degeneration = negative); OA = cartilage loss >50% thickness over >=~1 cm; effusion / Baker's = moderate or large only; MCL = high-grade partial or complete acute tear (low-grade sprain = negative). The same protocol presumably produced the 58 gold train rows and the hidden test labels **[?]**.
- **[P]** Hidden test ~1,300 studies (one repo says 1,322), public LB = 30 %, private = 70 % ([STRATEGY.md](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/homeshwarnelakurthi/RSNA-Knee-Abnormality-Detection/main/docs/STRATEGY.md), [TranBaDat2607 report](https://github.com/TranBaDat2607/RSNA-Knee-Abnormality-Detection)). The 3 rows in test.csv are placeholders. Whether the private set has the same series-type distribution as train is **[?]** – nobody has published a host statement on it; the only hint is the Data page warning that test DICOMs may use compressed transfer syntaxes.
- **[P]** Efficiency track: $18,000 of the pool. Formula quoted by two participants as the standard Kaggle one: `Efficiency = AUC / (Base - maxAUC) + RuntimeSeconds / 32400`, minimised, where Base = naive-baseline AUC and maxAUC = best private AUC. The structure matches Kaggle's published efficiency pages for other competitions ([Predict Student Performance](https://www.kaggle.com/competitions/predict-student-performance-from-game-play/overview/efficiency-prize-evaluation)). Implication (with Base≈0.5, maxAUC≈0.95): **1 h of runtime ≈ 0.05 AUC; 0.01 AUC ≈ 12 min**. Verify on the Overview page before final planning.
- **[P]** Rules: participants quote rule 4.b (no making competition data available to non-participants) as the reason not to send reports to hosted LLM APIs; the host had not ruled definitively at the time of writing. External click-through datasets (MRNet, OAI, fastMRI+) were also "unruled – treat as blocked" ([STRATEGY.md](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/homeshwarnelakurthi/RSNA-Knee-Abnormality-Detection/main/docs/STRATEGY.md)). Several public label tables were nevertheless produced with OpenAI models (files named `llm_labels_gpt54mini.csv`, `labels_llm_gpt56sol.csv`) **[V, file names only]**.

### Dataset statistics reported by participants (all [P]; re-verify on our copy)
- 4,407 studies / 24,371 series / 819,078 files; 3–14 series per study (median 5); 11–320 slices per series (median 30, p99 160) ([JunhaoLiXD](https://github.com/JunhaoLiXD/RSNA_Knee_Abnormality_Detection), [FINDINGS.md](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/homeshwarnelakurthi/RSNA-Knee-Abnormality-Detection/main/docs/FINDINGS.md)).
- `Fluid_Sensitive` and `Fat_Suppression` reported as perfectly correlated, so only 6 plane×sequence "slots" exist; four cover >94 % of studies, axial non-fluid appears in only 19.4 %; axial fluid-sensitive covers 100 % of studies ([RESEARCH_AGENDA.md](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/homeshwarnelakurthi/RSNA-Knee-Abnormality-Detection/main/docs/RESEARCH_AGENDA.md)).
- Physical scale: FOV median 160 mm (130–205), native pixel spacing median 0.312 mm, ~3.4x variation across the corpus; a 130 mm crop covers 99.6 % of series ([FINDINGS.md](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/homeshwarnelakurthi/RSNA-Knee-Abnormality-Detection/main/docs/FINDINGS.md)).
- Laterality tag missing in ~50 % of series/studies; recoverable from image geometry (97.4 % agreement) except GE (no tag) and Hitachi (n=8, inverted); 25 bilateral studies (0.57 %); all 4,407 PatientIDs distinct. SeriesDescription is among the surviving tags but only 82 % usable (Siemens 100 % placeholders).
- One repo claims every train series is uncompressed Explicit VR LE with 5.2 ms/slice decode; this **contradicts our own verification of mixed transfer syntaxes** – treat their number as a lower bound only.
- Reports: 9 language groups (English 39.4 %, Turkish 12.4 %, Spanish 12.1 %, then Croatian, Greek, German, Bulgarian, Dutch, French); median ~129 words; de-identification placeholders present; French has no gold-labelled example ([RoroDev2023](https://github.com/RoroDev2023/knee-mri-weak-supervision)).
- **Reporting bias vs gold labels**: synovitis positive in 46.6 % of gold studies but mentioned in 11.9 % of reports; fracture 31.0 % vs 4.9 %. Report-derived labels agree with gold only ~82 % ([FINDINGS.md](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/homeshwarnelakurthi/RSNA-Knee-Abnormality-Detection/main/docs/FINDINGS.md)). Consequence: "not mentioned" ≠ negative for several labels.
- Metadata-only model reaches 0.598 AUC under site-grouped folds; random K-fold overstates AUC by ~0.053 via scanner memorisation ([homeshwarnelakurthi README](https://github.com/homeshwarnelakurthi/RSNA-Knee-Abnormality-Detection)).

## 2. Community approaches for this competition

**Label extraction from reports (the acknowledged bottleneck):**
- Rule-based multilingual extractors (pilkwang `report_labels_v2.csv`, JunhaoLiXD): 3 states per label (explicit positive / explicit negative / unmentioned). Gold-58 agreement AUC ≈ 0.81 ([TranBaDat2607](https://github.com/TranBaDat2607/RSNA-Knee-Abnormality-Detection)).
- Local open-weight LLMs: Qwen2.5-3B-Instruct with 0/1/null output, 93.7 % coverage, 80.3 % agreement ([RoroDev2023](https://github.com/RoroDev2023/knee-mri-weak-supervision)); MedGemma-1.5-4b-it failed the JSON contract 0/60 vs Qwen 58/60 ([nttssv](https://github.com/nttssv/rsna-knee-medgemma)). Hosted GPT labelling: agreement AUC 0.869 vs 0.814 for rules ([TranBaDat2607](https://github.com/TranBaDat2607/RSNA-Knee-Abnormality-Detection)); the best single public LLM table (stevenleehans `llm_labels_v2.csv`) reaches 0.887 gold-58 macro AUC, three-way exact consensus **hurts** (0.836) because it discards synovitis/effusion signal ([ShivenKhurana1 audit](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/ShivenKhurana1/rsna-knee-abnormality/main/v15/LABEL_CONSENSUS_AUDIT.md)).
- 4-state extraction (affirmed / hedged / negated / absent) mapped to soft targets (1.0 / calibrated / 0.1 / 0.28) raised gold agreement from 80.7 % to 86.5 % ([vhittuvalli](https://github.com/vhittuvalli/RSNA-Knee-Abnormality-Detection)). Fold-local empirical-Bayes calibration of state→probability on the gold studies of the training folds, with a positive > unmentioned > negative ordering constraint, fixed a "collapse to base rate" failure ([JunhaoLiXD](https://github.com/JunhaoLiXD/RSNA_Knee_Abnormality_Detection)). Severity-graded targets (report severity ≈ probability two MSK readers would call it positive) are the recurring "central thesis".
- **Pitfall:** at least five public report-label tables copy the 58 gold rows verbatim, silently inflating any gold-58 validation that uses them ([TranBaDat2607 report](https://github.com/TranBaDat2607/RSNA-Knee-Abnormality-Detection)). Always drop gold rows from any external table before validating.

**Vision models:**
- Public reference ("DINOsaur"/pilkwang lineage): 6 slots (3 planes × fluid/non-fluid), constant-mm crop (130 mm; 90 mm for the sagittal fluid slot to help menisci), 336 px, DINOv2-S/14 partially fine-tuned, 9 depth slices per slot fed as three RGB triplets, mean+max pooling over slots, per-diagnosis attention head, no horizontal flip (would swap medial/lateral), small-rotation TTA; single fold 0.841 LB, 5-fold 0.821 ([vhittuvalli](https://github.com/vhittuvalli/RSNA-Knee-Abnormality-Detection)).
- The ~0.94 ensembles are dominated by a **CoAtNet-RMLP-2 @384, 2.5-D attention-MIL** family ("Raptor" checkpoints v4/v5/v8/v10; 64 slices per series, 2–98 % intensity windowing), blended with a residual-gated CoAtNet and DINOv2/CNN+BiGRU arms; 4 arms with fixed weights = 0.939 in 2.5–3 h of the 9 h budget; adding DINOv2/RadImageNet arms gave +0.002; backbone swaps on identical labels/views gave +0.001 ([TranBaDat2607](https://github.com/TranBaDat2607/RSNA-Knee-Abnormality-Detection), [public_score_audit](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/ShivenKhurana1/rsna-knee-abnormality/main/research/public_score_audit.md)). Training that family took 5–7 h on 2x T4.
- Per-label weak spots in the best public pipeline (gold-58): synovitis 0.83, PF OA 0.85, lateral OA 0.86, lateral meniscus 0.88; medial findings 0.97–0.98. Weak models also fail on MCL and medial meniscus (below chance with 6 slices/plane) – small-structure labels need more slices and resolution.
- OrthoDiffusion (MSK-MRI diffusion foundation model, 15,948 knee MRIs, [arXiv 2602.20752](https://arxiv.org/abs/2602.20752)) has public weights on Hugging Face and a Kaggle re-upload, but the advertised effusion AUC 0.958 was not reproducible (0.883 measured) ([diagnosis](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/ShivenKhurana1/rsna-knee-abnormality/main/v11/orthodiffusion_diagnosis.md)) – low priority.

**Validation and known pitfalls:** grouped folds by site proxy (language|manufacturer|model → 75 groups) and report grouped + random CV side by side; canonicalise laterality (5 of 12 labels are side-specific); geometric slice sorting, not filename order; AUC on soft targets needs binarisation; pretrained weights silently fall back to random init offline (one repo scored 0.487 for that reason); LB-tuned per-target weights overfit; "pipeline/label diversity beats more backbones".

Common denominator of every winner: 2D ImageNet backbone on 3-slice stacks at 384–512 px, a sequence/attention head over slices, max/attention over series, heavy ensembling by *view/label diversity* rather than backbone count.

## 4. Kaggle offline mechanics (2026)

- **Weights:** Kaggle Models and Datasets both mount under `/kaggle/input/...` and work with internet off ([Kaggle Models docs](https://www.kaggle.com/docs/models)). Datasets are simpler for a bundle of `.pt/.safetensors` + code; Models add versioning/licence metadata. Load timm backbones from local files (`timm.create_model(name, pretrained=False)` + `load_state_dict`, or `pretrained_cfg_overlay=dict(file=...)`) and **assert** the load – silent random init already burned one team.
- **Packages:** upload wheels as a dataset and `pip install --no-index --find-links=/kaggle/input/<wheels> pkg` ([example](https://www.kaggle.com/code/xavierreyrobert/package-wheels-in-dataset-no-internet-pip-install)). Build wheels against the Kaggle image's Python/CUDA (check `!pip list` in a live kernel first; the image changes).
- **DICOM decoding:** pydicom + `pylibjpeg` (`pylibjpeg-libjpeg` for JPEG Lossless, `pylibjpeg-openjpeg` for JPEG 2000) or `python-gdcm` cover all our syntaxes ([pydicom plugin table](https://pydicom.github.io/pydicom/stable/guides/plugin_table.html)); `dicomsdl` is a fast C++ reader popular in RSNA breast 2023; GPU JPEG-2000 via nvImageCodec/nvjpeg2k or DALI gave 5–17x on J2K in RSNA breast ([nvImageCodec DICOM sample](https://docs.nvidia.com/cuda/nvimagecodec/samples/DICOM-pydicom.html), [DALI notebook](https://www.kaggle.com/code/tivfrvqhs5/decode-jpeg2000-dicom-with-dali)) but only for J2K. No published apples-to-apples ms/slice benchmark for our mix **[?]** – measure on our data (uncompressed ≈ 5 ms/slice on Kaggle CPU per one participant).
- **Budget arithmetic:** ~1,320 studies × ~5 series × ~30 slices ≈ 200k slices; at 5–20 ms/slice on 4 cores → 4–17 min decode if parallelised; a single 384 px 2.5-D model at ~0.5–1 s/study on one T4 → 10–20 min; 4-arm public ensembles run 2.5–3 h. Hard limit 9 h; keep ≥25 % margin for long-tail studies (320-slice series).
- **Efficiency score:** with the formula in §1, a 20-min / 0.90 model beats a 3-h / 0.92 one; a fast single model can be entered for both tracks.

## 5. Backbones (timm, ImageNet-pretrained) and domain-specific weights

- Proven in this competition: `coatnet_rmlp_2_rw_384` (the 0.94 family), DINOv2-S/14 (`vit_small_patch14_dinov2`, partial fine-tune), ResNet-34/50, EfficientNet-B0 (weak).
- Proven in RSNA 2022–2024 2.5-D winners: `tf_efficientnetv2_s`, `convnext_tiny/small` (v1 and `convnextv2_tiny`), `coat_lite_medium`, `coatnet_1_rw_224`, `maxvit_tiny_tf_384/512`, `efficientnet_b4/b6` (localisers), `resnet50d`/`resnet18` (seg/crop stages). MaxViT and CoAtNet need fixed input sizes; ConvNeXt/EfficientNet are resolution-flexible and fp16-friendly on T4.
- Knee-specific pretrained weights: MRNet weights are not officially released; OrthoDiffusion weights are public but unreproduced (§2); PRISM multi-sequence MRI FM ([arXiv 2508.07165](https://arxiv.org/abs/2508.07165)) – knee coverage and weight availability unconfirmed **[?]**; RadImageNet arms added ~0 in public ensembles. **Recommendation: ImageNet-pretrained 2D backbones fine-tuned on our weak labels; do not depend on domain FMs.**

## 6. Recommendations for the architect (prioritised)

1. **Own label teacher before any big vision run.** Run an open-weight multilingual LLM locally on the A6000s (Qwen2.5/Qwen3 7B–32B-Instruct class; small 3–4B models were unreliable) with a per-label 4-state + severity schema (affirmed/hedged/negated/absent, plus grade), few-shot in each language, JSON-constrained decoding, two prompt variants + rule-based extractor as extra voters; keep disagreement as uncertainty (soft target / down-weight), not consensus. Validate on gold-58 **after removing gold rows from any public table**. Target: >0.89 gold-58 macro AUC (public best 0.887). Do not use hosted APIs (rule 4.b risk).
2. **Severity-aware soft targets, fold-local calibration, per-label masking.** Map states/grades to probabilities calibrated on training-fold gold only; for under-reported labels (synovitis, fracture, Baker's, contusion) treat "unmentioned" as missing/low-weight rather than negative; weight gold rows ~5–10x.
3. **Vision core = 6-slot 2.5-D attention-MIL.** Per study select one series per slot (plane × fluid), crop a constant 130 mm FOV (tighter 90–100 mm sagittal crop as a meniscus specialist), 384 px, 3-slice stacks over ≥16–32 sampled slices, shared 2D backbone, attention pooling over slices, then attention/concat over slots with presence masks; no horizontal flip; canonicalise laterality (DICOM tag → geometry fallback). Start with `convnext_small` or `tf_efficientnetv2_s` (fast, fp16), add `coatnet_rmlp_2_rw_384` and `maxvit_tiny_tf_384` as diverse arms later.
4. **Validation = site-grouped CV + gold-58 as sanity holdout, reported side by side with random CV.** Group key: manufacturer|model|language (or a stronger scanner fingerprint if available). Expect ~0.03 noise on gold-58; use OOF on the full weak-labelled corpus for decisions and gold-58 only as a leak check.
5. **Borrow RSNA-2024 tricks for noisy labels:** manifold mixup on embeddings, confident-learning denoising with OOF predictions, second-round pseudo-labels (0.5 real + 0.5 pseudo), EMA, small-rotation TTA. Privileged text head (report embedding as auxiliary target, dropped at inference) is a cheap extra.
6. **Efficiency track as a first-class deliverable:** one fast model (≤30 min on T4x2, decode parallelised over 4 cores, fp16, channels_last), fixed early; the heavier ensemble is a separate submission.
7. **Inference hardening:** decode by actual transfer syntax (pylibjpeg + gdcm fallback), geometric slice sort, missing-slot handling, `/kaggle/temp` creation, asserts on weight loading, rehearsal on a synthetic ~1,300-study manifest; time budget with 25 % margin.
8. **Do not chase the public LB**; it is compressed (0.935 = rank ~200) and ~390 studies. Diversity across labels/views/teachers, not per-target LB weight tuning.

### Open questions for the analyst (answer from our data copy)

- Transfer-syntax distribution by manufacturer/site; decode ms/slice per syntax with pydicom+pylibjpeg vs gdcm vs dicomsdl on our machine; do the 3 public test studies differ from train in syntax or slot mix?
- Which of the 86 tags survive (Laterality, ImagePositionPatient/IOP, PixelSpacing, Manufacturer/Model/Software, PatientSex)? Laterality coverage and geometric-recovery agreement; bilateral count.
- Gold-58: language, site, per-label prevalence (vs report-mention rates); any duplicated PatientIDs.
- Report language and length distribution; share of reports with severity words; placeholder/de-identification artefacts; any non-knee or post-operative reports.
- FOV / pixel spacing distribution to confirm the 130 mm / 384 px choice; per-plane slice thickness.
